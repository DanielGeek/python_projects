# Plan: Premium coffee landing page

## Palette (colors taken from the image)

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cup black | ■ | `#141210` | Main background and strong text (warm black, not pure black) |
| Walnut wood | ■ | `#5C3414` | Section backgrounds, footer, hover states |
| Caramel cream | ■ | `#D4AC66` | Accent: buttons, prices, details |
| Foam | ■ | `#F2EEE6` | Light background and text on dark backgrounds |
| Charcoal gray | ■ | `#2E2C29` | Cards on the black, borders, dividers |

**Usage rule:** 60% dark (black or wood), 30% foam and 10% caramel. Caramel is used only for things you want the user to click.

## Typography

- **Headings:** a serif with character, such as Playfair Display or DM Serif Display.
- **Body text:** a neutral sans, such as Work Sans or DM Sans.

## Required pages

1. **Home (landing)**: the main page (detailed below).
2. **Shop / Catalog**: filters by origin, roast, grind and format (whole bean, ground or capsule).
3. **Product page**: gallery, tasting notes, origin, roast, weight and grind selector, price, add-to-cart button and related products.
4. **Subscription**: choose the coffee, frequency and quantity, with the savings clearly visible.
5. **Cart**: side panel or page.
6. **Checkout**: details, shipping, payment and summary.
7. **Order confirmation.**
8. **About us / Origin**: the story, the producers and the roasting process.
9. **Brewing guide**: methods such as espresso, V60 or French press (helps SEO).
10. **User account**: orders, subscriptions and addresses.
11. **Contact and FAQ.**
12. **Legal pages**: shipping, returns, privacy and terms.

## Home structure

1. **Header**: logo, menu (Shop, Subscription, About us, Guide) and cart.
2. **Hero**: the client's photo at full screen over the black, a short headline and the "Buy coffee" button in caramel.
3. **Benefits strip**: fresh roast, free shipping from X and direct origin, with 3 or 4 simple icons.
4. **Featured coffees**: 3 or 4 cards with photo, name, tasting notes and price.
5. **How the subscription works**: 3 steps and a button.
6. **Story or origin**: image and short text on a wood background.
7. **Testimonials or reviews.**
8. **Quick brewing guide** with a link to the full guide.
9. **Newsletter** with an incentive, for example 10% off the first purchase.
10. **Footer**: links, social media, accepted payments and legal.

## Components the other AI must define

- Buttons: primary (caramel), secondary (foam outline) and text.
- Product card, weight and grind selector, quantity counter.
- Roast labels (light, medium, dark) and badges ("New", "Best seller").
- Inputs and form states, "added to cart" notice.
- Mobile version of everything: most coffee purchases are made from a phone.

## Image style

Warm photos with side lighting, wood, dark surfaces and close-ups of the crema and the beans. Keep this tone across all product photos.
